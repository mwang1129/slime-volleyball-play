          How to play

          Keep the ball off your side of the court. Move under it, jump to change the angle, and send it over the net into an open space.

          The basic rules

          • When the ball lands on your side, your opponent wins a point.
          • The first player to six points wins; you do not need to win by two.
          • You can touch the ball as many times as you like. Wall rebounds are allowed.
          • You can score whether or not you served. The winner of each point serves next.
          • Each slime stays on its own side of the net. Ducking makes your hitbox shorter, including during a jump.

          Match points and bonus scores

          Each rally is worth one match point. Bonus scores are separate; they never change the first-to-six match result.

          Computer campaign

          Beat White Slime, Angry Red Slimons, Slime Master, Psycho Slime, and Big Blue Boss in order. A loss ends your run. At the end, your accumulated score is multiplied by 2 for each level beyond the first: ×1, ×2, ×4, ×8, or ×16. Psycho moves faster and jumps higher; the Boss has a larger body.
